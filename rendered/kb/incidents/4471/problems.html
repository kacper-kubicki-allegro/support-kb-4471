The three problems

parser · the shared parser reads day-first dates month first · fixed

Every tenant on file writes dates day first. The shared parser read every date month first, so the day and the month swapped: “Booked for 2 December. The invite says 12 February.” It came in by app, by email, and through booking-v2 forms, whose hint asks for DD/MM/YYYY. This is the bug #4471’s title names. Fixed by fix(dates): parse dd/mm for eu locales. The fix names EU locales; harbour.dental (en-GB) is day-first too, so check its next invites: day-first is a question for the locale, not for a list of countries.

hint · booking-v3 asks for the month first · won’t fix

booking-v3 prints MM/DD/YYYY under its date field. People at day-first tenants on booking-v3 typed the month first, as the hint told them, and the form read the date the tenant’s way, day first. Same swap, so subject-line triage merged it into #4471, but the parser fix does not touch it. Won’t fix: the booking-v3 form is retired. Reports stop when its last tenants move off it.

one-day · a date moves by one day · open

Some reports are not a swap at all. “Invite says Tuesday 12 May. Calendar says Monday 11 May.” “Client in Lisbon got Tuesday. Client in Toronto got Monday. Same meeting.” “The invite arrived before I made the booking.” A day/month swap never moves a date by exactly one day, so neither the parser fix nor retiring booking-v3 closes these. A different day for people in different time zones, and one tenant who sees it “only in June”, point at a date kept as midnight and converted through another time zone or a daylight-saving offset. Nobody owns it yet. It needs its own investigation.

How the reports were counted

  • One id, one report. The ticket export comes in overlapping pages, so some reports appear twice. Each id is counted once.
  • The tenant decides, not the intake tag. Intake tags a report from the reporter’s browser. The cause depends on the tenant’s locale and booking form (causes.ts, locales.ts).
  • Counted as the desk counts. Every report is counted once, where its tenant files it, exactly as the #4471 table counts it. The table checks that sum against the ticket.
  • A one-day move is its own problem. The desk already read these apart (desk-notes.json). They stay counted where their tenant files them, as the #4471 footnote says, and they also get their own row and their own ticket. Each report body was checked: every other report names a swapped day and month, or is consistent with one.
  • The numbers come from the data at render time (problems.ts, ProblemTable.svelte), so they stay right when the desk merges more reports.

What this changes for the desk

  • #4471 was not one known issue, and closing it did not close the one-day move. Triage new date reports to the ticket that matches their cause.
  • The subject line cannot tell these problems apart: the same subjects (“Wrong day on the invite”, “Date is wrong”, “Month and day swapped?”) come in for all three. Subject-line dedupe (decision 0001) planned for one bug under different subjects; here it is different bugs under the same subjects. Splitting needs the tenant and the report body. That needs a new decision record; 0001 stays as it is.
