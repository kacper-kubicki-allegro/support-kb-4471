The three problems

parser · the shared parser reads day-first dates month first

Every tenant on file writes dates day first. The shared parser reads every date month first, so the day and the month swap: “Booked for 2 December. The invite says 12 February.” It comes in by app, by email, and through booking-v2 forms, whose hint asks for DD/MM/YYYY. This is the bug #4471’s title names, and the one the queued fix is for.

hint · booking-v3 asks for the month first

booking-v3 prints MM/DD/YYYY under its date field. People at day-first tenants on booking-v3 type the month first, as the hint tells them, and the form reads the date the tenant’s way, day first. The symptom is the same swap, so subject-line triage merged it into #4471. The parser fix does not touch it: the hint has to show the tenant’s own order, or the form has to read the order its hint asks for. It only comes in through booking-v3 forms.

one-day · a date moves by one day

Some reports are not a swap at all. “Invite says Tuesday 12 May. Calendar says Monday 11 May.” “Client in Lisbon got Tuesday. Client in Toronto got Monday. Same meeting.” “The invite arrived before I made the booking.” A day/month swap never moves a date by exactly one day, so the parser fix cannot close these. A different day for people in different time zones, and one tenant who sees it “only in June”, point at a date kept as midnight and converted through another time zone or a daylight-saving offset. It needs its own investigation.

How the reports were counted

  • The tenant decides, not the intake tag. Intake tags a report from the reporter’s browser. The cause depends on the tenant’s locale and booking form (causes.ts, locales.ts).
  • A one-day move is its own problem. The desk already read these apart (desk-notes.json); the incident table still counts them under parser and footnotes them. Here they get their own row. Each report body was checked: every other report names a swapped day and month, or is consistent with one.
  • Every report is in exactly one problem. The table checks the sum against the ticket.
  • The numbers come from the data at render time (problems.ts, ProblemTable.svelte), so they stay right when the desk merges more reports.
